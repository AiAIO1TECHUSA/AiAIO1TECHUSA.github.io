Original Demand Letter Text

As my clients have now completed all necessary medical treatment for injuries sustained in the referenced accident, this letter serves as a formal and final demand for settlement of their injuries.

This correspondence is submitted solely for settlement purposes and may not be used for any other intent without the express written consent of this office.

Liability

The subject incident occurred on December 25, 2025, as my clients' vehicle was traveling northbound on I-15 when their vehicle was struck in the rear as it stopped for traffic.

Following an investigation, Mr. Alpha was cited for failure to decrease speed (citation #11009141).

Damages

Our clients were admitted to UMC for emergency treatment and diagnosis. The emergency room physician, Dr. Bentley Doc, ordered cervical spine and lumbar spine x-rays and administered treatment.

On December 29, 2025 our clients sought further treatment at Desert Inn Medical Center with Mark Doc, D.O. and Frank Doc.

Based on the findings and the physical examinations, the following diagnosis of the injuries sustained by Mr. & Mrs. Q as a result of the subject accident were made:

  • SEVERE THORACIC and CERVICAL NECK STRAIN/SPRAIN
  • BILATERAL TRAPEZIUS MUSCLE STRAIN/SPRAIN
  • BILATERAL UPPER EXTREMITY RADICULOPATHY, numbness/tingling, worse on right
  • Mild bilateral sensory deficit

Mr. Q has treated with pain management modalities, chiropractic manipulative therapy, and medication. Intramuscular injection was administered because ongoing neck pain persisted.
